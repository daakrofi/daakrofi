# Daniel Akrofi

I'm a PhD researcher at Warwick Business School with a background in commercial strategy. I use NLP and statistical modelling to study how people talk about products before launch, and I build tools that make the evidence easier to explore.

[Portfolio and CV](https://daakrofi.github.io/meet-daniel-akrofi/) | [Warwick profile](https://www.wbs.ac.uk/about/person/1950442/) | [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/daniel-ansah-akrofi)

## Doctoral Research

**7.96 million Steam posts and comments across 1,695 games.** I aligned discussion and market signals to launch dates, fine-tuned DistilRoBERTa models to detect speculation and uncertainty, and checked the pipeline against 500 independently annotated sentences. Holding out entire publishers in cross-validation showed that a speculation measure improved sales and revenue forecasts over standard measures of buzz.

[Research case study](research/steam-launch-analysis.md) | [500-sentence validation exercise](https://github.com/daakrofi/prus-human-validation-sentence-components) | [Steam data collection utility](https://github.com/daakrofi/Steam-Data-Collection)

My current study uses a difference-in-differences event-study design and Bayesian structural time-series modelling to examine how pre-release announcements change what players express uncertainty about.
